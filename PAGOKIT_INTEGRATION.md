- ✅ `checkout.session.expired` — no-op (premium was never granted)

## ⚠️ Important limitation: this does NOT cover Google Play Billing

`components/viajes/premium.ts` originally had a placeholder comment planning "Google Play
Billing" for when the app publishes to Google Play. **This integration is web-only.**

If Bitácora de Viaje ever ships as an installable Android app listed on Google Play, and the
premium unlock is purchased *from inside that app*, Google Play policy requires **Google Play
Billing** for digital content — a card processor like Stripe is not allowed there and the app
listing can get rejected. PagoKit's own catalog explicitly blocks Stripe (and every other card
PSP it builds) for that use case and only advises RevenueCat as an entitlement layer — it does
not generate that code. Implementing Play Billing is separate native Android work, out of
scope for this repo.

The Stripe flow above is safe to use **now**, before the app is on Google Play, or for any
purchase made from the web version outside the Play Store listing.
